# Pilot venue setup (target: under an hour)

Bring: the venue's menu with prices, number of tables per area, staff names, the owner's
settlement bank account (NUBAN), a printer (A4), scissors/laminator or table stands, and the
tablets/phones the staff will use (charged, on the venue Wi-Fi).

| Time    | Step                                                                                                                                                                                               | Who      |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| 0–5 min | Owner signs up at the staff app (`/signup`) on their phone                                                                                                                                         | Owner    |
| 5–10    | **Settings**: pay at the end (running tab) or pay first; VAT % and whether menu prices include it; service charge %; logo                                                                          | Owner    |
| 10–12   | **Payouts**: bank + account number → confirm the account name → save                                                                                                                               | Owner    |
| 12–15   | **Stations**: Kitchen, Bar (add Grill/Shisha if they have separate screens)                                                                                                                        | Manager  |
| 15–35   | **Menu**: categories, items with price and station, add-ons (e.g. "Extra plantain +₦500", "With ice"); photos optional (can be added later)                                                        | Manager  |
| 35–40   | **Tables**: "Add many tables" per area (e.g. M1–M10 Main Hall, T1–T6 Terrace); **Download QR sheet**, print, cut, place                                                                            | Manager  |
| 40–45   | **Staff**: each waiter and each station screen with a PIN; managers with email if they want to sign in from their phone                                                                            | Manager  |
| 45–50   | **Devices**: on each tablet/phone, sign in as owner/manager → Staff → _Enable PIN login on this device_ → Sign out. Station tablets: PIN in → Station → _Start Kitchen/Bar_ (turns on the sound)   | Manager  |
| 50–60   | **Test run**: waiter opens a table, someone scans and orders a water, kitchen/bar accept → ready, waiter serves, pay ₦100 online and record the rest as cash, close the table, check the dashboard | Everyone |

Brief the staff (5 minutes):

- Waiters: tap a table to **open** it when guests sit; customers cannot order before that.
  Watch "Customers asking" and "Ready to serve" on the Floor. Close the table when paid.
- Stations: **Accept** or **Unavailable** (also takes it off the menu; Undo if tapped by mistake),
  then **Ready**. Keep the screen on and the volume up.
- Managers: voids and discounts need your PIN and a reason; all are on the owner's dashboard.

After the first night: review **Dashboard** (sales by method, voids/discounts, refunds due) and
the **Audit log** with the owner.
